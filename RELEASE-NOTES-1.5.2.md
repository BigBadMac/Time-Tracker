# Time Tracker 1.5.2 - release notes (FULL DRAFT, for Brent to trim)

## Choose how often sync runs
- New: Account > Sync this board > How often. Four choices, each showing roughly what it uses in a working month:
  - Right away - changes go up within seconds; checks every minute while open (about 10 MB a month).
  - Smart - quick while you're working, slows to every 15 minutes when things are quiet (about 2 MB). This is where every device starts.
  - Every 15 minutes - sends and fetches together, four times an hour (about 1 MB).
  - When I open it - only when you open the app, come back to it, or tap Sync now (under 0.5 MB).
- Every choice still takes a fresh look when you open the app or come back to it.
- Each device keeps its own choice - your iPad and phone can differ.
- How often also shows what this device has really used so far this month.
- Sync now uses about half the data it did: each round no longer sets off a needless second one.

## Reminders
- A new reminder starts with the item's name as what to remind you about. Keep it, or just start typing to replace it.

## Sign-in
- The code boxes now start at eight, matching the code in the email. Six-digit codes still work.

## Also
- The manual's sync guide explains How often and what each choice costs.
