# Time Tracker 1.4 - release notes (FULL DRAFT, for Brent to trim)

## Sign in
- Sign in with your email: type it, then enter the code that arrives. No password to remember.
- The account sits at the foot of the menu, and a small cloud in the top bar shows how it stands. Tap the cloud to open it.
- The cloud's five looks:
  - grey ? - account not set up yet (it fades in and out now and then);
  - green check - signed in and checked in with the server;
  - blue turning arrows - checking in;
  - yellow ! - can't reach the server (it gives a little shake);
  - red Zz - offline or signed out.
- Signing in doesn't move your data yet. Everything stays on this device as before. Sync comes in a later release.
- Sign-in details are never put in backups.

## Reminders
- Projects and tasks with an end date can remind you before they end: switch on "Before it ends" under Remind me. It uses your default times, or tap Change to pick times for that one record.
- Times to choose from: at the time, an hour before, the start of that shift, a day before, a week before.
- Milestone steps can remind you before they're due.
- Add your own reminders to any project or task: a name, then a date and time, the start of your shift, or just before your shift ends. Repeat once, daily, on workdays or weekly. Shift reminders follow your Work Schedule.
- Reminders go off while the app is open. Any that came due while it was closed wait in "While you were away", and the app icon shows how many. Done clears the list; Later keeps it for next time.

## Nudges
- Time Settings > Tracking > Reminders & nudges: set your default reminder times, hold reminders until your shift starts, and turn nudges on or off:
  - not clocked in (15 minutes into your shift, by default);
  - a job still running when the shift ends;
  - one job running long (off by default);
  - still clocked in after the shift.

## Fixes and small things
- Long job names in the top bar end in "..." instead of running under the Break tag.
- The manual has two new guides: Sign in, and Reminders & nudges.
- The practice board never sends reminders.
