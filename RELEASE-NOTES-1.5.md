# Time Tracker 1.5 - release notes (FULL DRAFT, for Brent to trim)

## Sync your board between your devices
- New: your board can now follow you to every device you sign in on. It's off until you turn it on: Account > Sync this board.
- Only you can see your board on the server. Nobody else using the app can read it, not even an organization admin.
- What travels: projects, tasks and lanes; time, breaks and disruptions; reminders; your Work Schedule and settings; the running clock.
- What stays on each device: the look (theme, colours, fonts, light or dark, left or right hand), folded cards and lanes, and the practice board.
- Changes go up a moment after you make them and appear on your other devices within a minute, or straight away with Sync now. No reload needed.
- Offline? Changes wait on the device and go up when you're back online.
- When two devices change the same thing, the later change wins, detail by detail. Rename a project on one and change its lane on the other, and you keep both.
- The cloud in the top bar now shows real syncing: turning arrows while changes move, the green check when everything matches.

## Turning it on
- The first device simply uploads its board.
- On a device where the account already has a board, you're asked "Which board?":
  - Use the account's board (this device takes it);
  - Use this device's board (your other devices change to match);
  - Combine both (everything kept; a project made separately on each shows up twice).
- Both boards are counted so you can tell which is which, and a box says exactly what will happen.
- Replacing a board takes a hold, and a "Before sync" copy goes into Backups first.
- Turning sync off keeps everything on the device.

## Also
- The manual has a new guide: Sync your board between devices.
- Add-ons such as Tool Tracker sync too, when sync is on.
- With sync off, the app does no extra work and stores nothing extra.
- Ready for sharing: projects shared with other people (coming next) will stay out of your private sync, so a device on this version won't make a second copy of them.
