# Time Tracker 1.6 - release notes (FULL DRAFT, for Brent to trim)

## About Time Tracker
- New: Menu > User Manual > About Time Tracker.
- It shows which version you have, when it was built, and "Developed at Hiatt Studios".
- What's new lists the changes in this version; Version history lists every release back to 1.0.
- Privacy and your data says plainly what stays on your device, what goes to the server, and who can read it.

## Send feedback to the developer
- Send feedback: pick a problem, an idea, a question or praise, and say it in your own words.
- You need to be signed in, so a reply can reach you and nobody can send in your name.
- "Include app details" (on by default) adds the version, the kind of device, the look you use and how sync is set. The form shows exactly what will be sent. Never your board.
- A half-written note waits on your device if you close the sheet.

## My feedback
- Everything you've sent, each with where it stands: Sent, Looking into it, Needs your reply, Planned, Fixed in (version), or Closed.
- Replies from the developer show as a conversation, along with each change of status. You can write back.
- When a reply is waiting, About Time Tracker in the menu shows it, and My feedback marks it New reply.

## FAQ: questions people ask
- New: Menu > User Manual > FAQ. Questions grouped by topic; tap one to open its answer.
- "Show me how" opens the manual's guide for it.
- "Did this help? Yes / No" under each answer; No lets you say what's missing.
- "Still stuck? Send feedback" at the bottom.
- Anyone can read it, signed in or not, and it works offline once loaded.
- The manual's search finds FAQ answers too.
- On Send feedback, answers that look like a fit appear as you type a question or a problem, so you may not need to send it at all.

## For the developer and helpers (only the team sees this)
- About > Feedback inbox, with a count of what's new.
- Mail view: a quick list with Reply, Planned, Fixed and Close under each item. Blog view: whole conversations on one page. Switch with the toggle beside the title; the app remembers which you used.
- Each item: status, fixed in which version, priority, who it's assigned to, internal notes the sender never sees, and Add to FAQ.
- Team: add helpers by email; a helper sees only what's assigned to them.
- FAQ: write or edit questions, publish them, and review suggested questions drafted from the inbox.

## Signing in
- The code screen now shows all eight boxes, with "Input 6 or 8 digit code below" above them.
- The first six are solid; the last two are dashed, with "if your code has 8" under them. Type a seventh digit and they turn solid.
- Nothing on the screen moves as you type. Pasting the code, or tapping it above the keyboard, still signs you in straight away.

## Also
- The manual has a new guide: About Time Tracker, the FAQ and feedback.
